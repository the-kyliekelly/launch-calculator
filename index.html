✧ What To Do With These Numbers

A bigger list is not the only way to move the needle.

Every time you improve your open rate, click rate, or conversion rate, your required list size drops. A warm, nurtured list of 3,000 will consistently outperform a cold, neglected list of 30,000. Work on your rates before your next launch and the numbers start looking a lot friendlier.

Three things to focus on before your next launch:

→ Nurture before you sell. A warm list clicks. A cold list does not. Your pre-launch email sequence matters as much as your launch emails.
→ Write better subject lines. Open rate is the first domino. Everything else follows. Use the Subject Line Generator in this Treasure Chest.
→ Sharpen your calls to action. Is there one obvious thing to click in each email? Higher click rate means you need a smaller list to hit the same goal.
